1. **Complete Lifecycle Coverage** - Only platform covering Lead → Survey → BOM → Order → Dispatch → Install → Warranty → Service → Finance
2. **Indian Regulatory Compliance** - Built-in PM Surya Ghar CFA rules, GST 12%/18% split, Section 32 depreciation
3. **Technician-First Mobile** - Purpose-built field app with IEC 62446 checklists, canvas signature, serial scanning
4. **Unified Data Model** - Single customer identity across all 7 modules (no data silos)
5. **Warranty Automation** - Auto-activation on delivery, serial-linked, customer-visible
6. **Subsidy Ledger** - End-to-end PM Surya Ghar tracking (submitted → disbursed)

## Conclusion

**SunPower One establishes a new category: Solar ERP.**

Unlike single-focus tools (Aurora for CAD only, SolarEdge for monitoring only, Freyr for residential sales only), SunPower One is the **only platform** that bridges the entire solar installation business lifecycle from lead acquisition through technician sign-off, warranty certificate generation, and MNRE subsidy ledger tracking.

**Competitive Verdict:** SunPower One is not merely competitive—it defines a new category of Solar ERP that existing point solutions cannot match without significant architectural investment.
