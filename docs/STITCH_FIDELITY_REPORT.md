## 2. Typography & Material Scales

- **Headlines & Metric Data:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights 600, 700, 800) provides structural clarity and authoritative executive weight for numerical telemetry, kW ratings, and monetary values.
- **Body & Tabular Content:** `Inter` (weights 400, 500, 600) guarantees micro-legibility on dense data tables, Bill of Materials schedules, and technical inspection checklists.
- **Iconography:** `Material Symbols Outlined` + `Lucide-React` standardized to 16px/20px/24px grids.
